Who We Are

Zambian Owned.
Copper Belt Proven.

Inkaba Zambia Limited is a fully licensed Zambian company headquartered in Kitwe, Copperbelt Province. We specialise in mining contracting, civil construction, and industrial services for major copper producers and emerging operators across the country.

Our team brings deep regional knowledge, strong relationships with principal mining operators — including First Quantum Minerals, Barrick Lumwana, KCM, Mopani, NFC Africa, and CNMC — and the operational capacity to take on both large-scale and specialist contracts.

As a proudly Zambian business, we are committed to local employment, ZCCM-IH and ERB compliance, and building a legacy of excellence in the Copperbelt.

What We Do

Core Services

From underground development to surface civil works, Inkaba delivers with speed, safety, and precision.

01
Mining Contracting
Development headings, raise boring, shaft sinking, stope preparation, and production drilling for copper and other hard-rock mines across the Copperbelt and North-Western Province.
02
Civil Construction
Infrastructure and structural construction including mine surface facilities, processing plant civil works, roads, bridges, warehouses, and industrial buildings on and off site.
03
Earthworks & Bulk Excavation
Mass earthmoving, cut-and-fill, tailings dam construction, plant pad preparation, and open cast stripping using modern heavy equipment fleet, operated by qualified Zambian personnel.
04
Supply & Procurement
Strategic procurement and supply of mining consumables, PPE, ground support materials, explosives accessories, and capital equipment — with strong in-country and regional sourcing networks.
05
Plant & Equipment Hire
Flexible hire of excavators, dozers, graders, dump trucks, compactors, drills, and light vehicles — with or without operators — to supplement your project's equipment needs.
06
Project Management
Full-scope project planning, scheduling, QA/QC management, HSE oversight, and reporting — ensuring on-time, on-budget delivery from feasibility through to handover and commissioning.
